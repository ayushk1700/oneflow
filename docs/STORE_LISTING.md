# Store listing draft

## Title

ONEFLOW — Private Focus & Study Timer

## Short description

A private workspace in every new tab. Pick a task, start focusing, and see your progress. Works offline. No account needed.

## Long description

Make room for one thing. ONEFLOW turns each new tab into a private workspace for focused study and everyday work. Choose a scene, write one small task, and start your first session in a few clicks.

START EASILY — Set a task and choose your minutes. Your timer and task stay together, including when you open another tab. Switch between Home, Focus and Ambient without losing your session. Journal, Tasks, Sounds, Notes, Music, Habits and Stats open separately from the dock; scenery and appearance live in Settings.

STAY FOCUSED — Settle into a calm background, mix offline ambient sounds, or expand your session into Flow Mode. Optional website blocking follows your active Flow session. You control every connection and permission request.

SEE PROGRESS — Finish a session and see the time you spent on that task. Take a break, mark the task complete, or continue focusing. Review your focus history locally, without an account.

Enter Flow Mode for a fullscreen session with your current focus, timer and sound controls. Choose a Scene for deep work, a study cafe, creative time, evening wind-down or a minimal view. Save your own atmosphere and arrangement. Optional website blocking stays tied to running Flow focus sessions, with a gentle reminder and an explicit five-minute override.

Mix seven local sound beds, keep notes and gratitude entries, track habits/countdowns, check in with your energy, and review patterns calculated on your device. Daily rituals help you set an intention and reflect. Choose three priorities and compare estimates with recorded work.

ONEFLOW’s core workspace works offline without an account. Optional weather, Unsplash photographs and music need an internet connection. Search respects your browser’s default search engine. There are no analytics, ads or developer-operated collection. Export your data, optionally encrypt the backup, or delete it from Settings. Personal tasks and history stay in this browser profile; they do not automatically sync across devices.

Keyboard controls: N for a task, Space for the timer, F for Flow, / for search, Escape to close, Ctrl/Cmd+K for command search. Motion reduction, readable overlays and keyboard layout controls are included. English is primary; a Hindi sample falls back to English for untranslated text.

## Permission explanations

- Storage keeps records/preferences; browser sync contains only small settings.
- Search submits only searches you enter, through your browser’s existing default provider.
- Alarms/notifications support deadlines and optional reminders.
- Offscreen audio keeps your local mix playing after tabs close.
- Declarative Net Request applies temporary Flow rules. Website access is optional: selected sites for blocklists, all HTTP/HTTPS sites only for allow-only mode.
- Optional location/API hosts serve connected weather/photos. No history or cookie-reading permission.
