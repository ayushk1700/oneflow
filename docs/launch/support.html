A little help to keep going.

ONEFLOW is your private workspace in every new tab. Choose one task, start a session, and see your progress. Your core workspace works offline without an account.

Start and continue

Choose a scene and enter your first task in the welcome screen, or skip to the dashboard. Set your minutes and select Start focus. At the end, take a break, mark the task complete or continue focusing. Switch between Home, Focus and Ambient using the bottom-right controls. Journal, Tasks, Sounds, Notes, Music, Habits and Stats have separate dock buttons; other tools are grouped under Tools. Click the landscape caption or open Settings → Scenery to choose a background.

Save and restore

Open Settings → Data & privacy. Export a backup before changing profiles or uninstalling. On another browser, install ONEFLOW and select your backup from the same panel. Enter its passphrase if encrypted, then review the replacement confirmation. Lost passphrases cannot be recovered. Tasks and journals do not automatically sync between devices.

Search and connections

Search uses the browser’s default provider. Change it in browser settings. Weather, photos, dictionary and music are optional and need a connection. Denying permission keeps the core dashboard usable.

Timer or sound troubleshooting

A closed browser cannot deliver alarms or sound. Reopening reconciles elapsed time without inventing unattended sessions. Check your OS notification settings and Settings → Focus. If a problem repeats, describe what happened in Help & feedback; share a diagnostic export only if you choose.

Keyboard

N: new task. Space: start or pause. F: fullscreen focus. /: search. Esc: close. Ctrl/Cmd+K: find an action. These shortcuts pause while you type.

Feedback

Open Tools → Help & feedback, write a message and download it. Nothing is sent automatically. The file contains only your message, chosen topic and extension version. Share it through your publisher’s support channel or with your beta organizer.
